ChefVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 June 2026

ChefVault ("the App") is a recipe and kitchen-prep tool published by Winery Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Questions: mikael@thewinery.com.sg.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and the name you provide when you sign up. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email and name those services share. Optionally, a job title and avatar image.
  • Content you create — recipes (ingredients, steps, costs, photos), collections, and prep lists you add to the App.
  • Subscription status — whether your account is on the free or Pro plan. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see or store your payment-card details.
  • Recipe import — when you import a recipe from a link you provide (e.g. a social or web URL), that link and its public content are sent to third-party processors to extract the recipe text (see "Service providers").

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell your personal information.

How we use it

  • To provide and sync the App across your devices.
  • To enforce free-plan limits and unlock Pro features after a valid purchase.
  • To respond to support requests.

Service providers

We share information only with the providers needed to run the App:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage (hosting region: Singapore).
  • Apple and RevenueCat — subscription purchase and entitlement management.
  • Google (Gemini), Apify, and Firecrawl — used only when you import a recipe from a link, to extract recipe content from the page or video you submit.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated content from Settings → Security & Password → Delete Account in the App; this removes your recipes, collections, prep lists, stored images, and authentication record.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your sign-in session is stored in the device's secure storage (Apple Keychain on iOS).

Children

ChefVault is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can access and edit your profile in the App, export your data (Settings → Export all data), and delete your account at any time. To make other requests, contact us at the email above.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
