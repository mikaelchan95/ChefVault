Pan-Seared Salmon with Asparagus

Active
Cuisine French
Prep Time 15 mins
Cook Time 20 mins

Scaling & Yield

Adjusting weights automatically

4 Servings
restaurant

Ingredients

Qty Unit Ingredient
800 g Salmon Fillets (skin-on)
500 g Asparagus Spears (trimmed)
60 ml Extra Virgin Olive Oil
40 g Unsalted Butter
2 pc Lemons (zested & juiced)
10 g Sea Salt (Maldon)
assignment

Method

1

Pat the salmon fillets dry with paper towels. Season the skin side heavily with sea salt. Allow to temper at room temperature for 10 minutes before cooking.

2

Heat olive oil in a large stainless steel pan over medium-high heat until shimmering. Place salmon skin-side down and press gently with a spatula for 30 seconds to ensure even contact.

3

Reduce heat to medium. Cook for 6-7 minutes until skin is crispy and the flesh is cooked 75% through. Add butter and lemon juice to the pan.

4

Toss asparagus in the butter-lemon emulsion in the same pan. Flip salmon briefly (30 seconds) and remove from heat. Rest for 2 minutes before serving.
